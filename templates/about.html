Simra Chopra.

Simran Chopra is a trans-disciplinary professional who has been working in the fields of design, art and research to create thought-provoking experiences. Having graduated as a Communication Designer, she went on to pursue masters in New Media Design from the National Institute of Design. She intends her work to be a remark on everyday dilemmas, by utilising varied skills, processes and design principles. She believes that technology can be used as a medium to express, to understand people socially and psychologically to alter behaviours for a sustainable new world. This, mingled with her keen interest in gastronomical pursuits and exposure to new media technologies has sparked her desire to explore the fusion of the both.
