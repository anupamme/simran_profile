I am a multifaceted individual who likes to think, question, innovate and build. My interest and areas of work are varied and can range from being a cook, an investigator, a designer, an ethnographer, an urban farmer, a food activist, an educator, an artist or maybe more.

I have always been a person who likes to question and contemplate, early on in life. Having undergone my share of life experiences that have stayed on with me and become the foundation for anything I do. I like to build things that matter rather than just for fun or it just looks good. I care deeply about social justice especially in the areas of food, education and economics.

Professionally I like to work across design, art and research to create thought-provoking experiences. Graduate programme in New Media Design from the National Institute of Design reignited the tinkering child in me that I had lost somewhere. It introduced new perspectives that created a kaleidoscope of opportunities for me. The possibility to merge technology with art and design helps me create unique experiences. However, I like to question the role of technology in my work and society in general.

I intend my work to be a remark on everyday dilemmas, by utilising varied skills, processes and design principles. I believe that technology can be used as a medium to express, to understand people socially and psychologically to alter behaviours for a sustainable new world.

I have reached somewhere but the journey is long and the learning is vast.
